Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026/08/01

Data Storage

Project, client, quote, material, and company data, including related estimation records, are primarily stored on your own device for your management and use.

Camera and Photos Permissions

The app requests camera or photo-library permissions only when you actively use project photo features. These permissions are used only to take, select, and save project photos.

PDF Sharing

PDF previewing, sharing, printing, and sending are all initiated by you through system sharing features. The app does not send files or recipient information on its own.

Third-Party Services

The current version does not include third-party ads, analytics, or cloud sync and upload services. The app does not sell personal data.

Data Security

You are responsible for securing your device, backups, and shared content. If you share files with a third party through system sharing, that sharing is governed by the privacy policy of the service you choose.

Policy Updates

If this policy changes, the latest version and date will be updated on this page.
